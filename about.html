What we stand for

Picked, not scooped

Our pickers choose your produce the way they would choose their own — and call you before swapping anything.

Honest timing

The window we show is the window we keep. If we are running late, you hear it from us first.

Fair to everyone

Shelf prices, no surge on a rainy evening, and drivers paid for every trip they make.

How the service works

Every order is picked from a real store, not a warehouse on the edge of town. That is why the delivery window is short and the produce still looks like produce when it arrives.

  • You order — packets are sold in fixed sizes, so 1 kg is always 1 kg.
  • We pick — chilled and frozen items are packed last, in insulated bags.
  • We drive — you get the driver's number by SMS when he sets off.

Where we source from

Fruit and vegetables come from the Sulaibiya market most mornings, dairy and bakery from local producers, and the rest from the same distributors that supply Kuwait's supermarkets. Nothing sits on our shelves longer than it would on theirs.

Less waste, lighter bags

Orders are packed in reusable bags the driver takes back on his next visit, and produce close to its date goes to a daily discount shelf rather than a bin.
