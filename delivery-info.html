Delivery hours

We deliver seven days a week, from 8:00 AM to 11:00 PM Kuwait time. Orders placed after the last slot are delivered first thing the next morning.

How your order travels

  • Order Accepted — we have your order and payment is authorised.
  • Preparing — a picker walks the aisles and packs your items.
  • On the Way — the driver sets off and you get his number by SMS.

You can follow all three stages on the Orders page at any time.

Fresh and frozen items

Chilled and frozen goods travel in insulated bags and go into the van last, so they are the coldest items when they reach your door. If anything arrives warm, tell us and we replace it.

Substitutions

If a packet is out of stock, we call or message you before swapping it. Nothing is substituted without your say-so, and you are never charged more than the item you ordered.

If you are not home

The driver waits five minutes and calls the number on the address. After that the order returns to the store and we contact you to arrange a new slot.
