Returns & refunds

Groceries are perishable, so our rule is simple: if it is not right, we make it right.

Refund

Back to the card or KNET account you paid with.

Replacement

Sent free with your next delivery, or the same day if you need it.

Store credit

Applied instantly and used on your next order.

You have 24 hours

Tell us about a missing, damaged or spoiled item within 24 hours of delivery and we settle it the same day. For non-food items — household goods, cleaning products, anything sealed — the window is 7 days as long as the packaging is unopened.

How to report an item

  • Open the order on the Orders page and tap the item.
  • Say what went wrong and add a photo if the item is damaged.
  • Pick a refund, a replacement or store credit.

If you would rather talk to someone, message us on WhatsApp with the order number and we handle it from there.

When the money arrives

  • Store credit — instant.
  • KNET — 3 to 5 working days.
  • Visa / Mastercard / Apple Pay — 5 to 10 working days, depending on your bank.
  • Cash on delivery — refunded as store credit, or in cash at the store.

What we cannot take back

Fresh items that were opened or partly used, and chilled or frozen goods that were left out of the fridge, cannot be returned — we have no way to tell how they were stored. This does not affect anything that arrived damaged, spoiled or wrong: those are always our problem, not yours.

Cancelling an order

An order can be cancelled free of charge while it still shows Order Accepted. Once it moves to Preparing the shopping has started, so message us and we will cancel what we can.
