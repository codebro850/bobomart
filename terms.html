Terms of use

BoboMart is a grocery delivery service operating in the State of Kuwait. By placing an order you agree to these terms. If you do not agree with them, please do not use the service.

You must be 18 or older to hold an account. You are responsible for keeping your password to yourself and for every order placed from your account.

Orders & prices

All prices are shown in Kuwaiti Dinar (KD) and include applicable taxes. Prices and offers can change at any time, but the price you see at checkout is the price you pay for that order.

  • An order is confirmed once payment is authorised and you see the confirmation screen.
  • Deal of the Day prices apply only while the countdown is running.
  • Delivery is free on orders of 100 KD or more; below that the fee shown at checkout applies.
  • We may limit the quantity of any single item per order.

Returns, refunds and cancellations are covered by our returns policy, which forms part of these terms.

Privacy

We collect only what a grocery order needs: your name, email, mobile number, delivery addresses and order history. Card details are handled by our payment gateway and are never stored on our own servers.

What we use it for

  • Delivering your order and letting you track it.
  • Answering support messages about an order.
  • Sending offers — only if you opted in, and you can switch it off at any time.

We share data with the delivery driver assigned to your order and with our payment provider. We do not sell your data to anyone.

Cookies & local storage

The site keeps your chosen language, your cart and your session in your browser so the app works between visits. Nothing in that storage identifies you to anyone else.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of your data, correct anything that is wrong, or delete your account entirely from the account details page. Deleting the account removes your addresses and order history and cannot be undone.

Contact

Questions about these terms or your data? Message us from the help page and we reply within a few hours.

Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the State of Kuwait, and the courts of Kuwait have jurisdiction over any dispute arising from them.
