Frequently asked

How fast is delivery?

Express orders arrive in 15–30 minutes. Normal delivery arrives within 2 hours, and the evening slot lands between 6:00 and 9:00 PM.

When is delivery free?

Delivery is free on every order above 100 KD. Below that a small fee applies, shown on the payment step before you pay.

Which payment methods can I use?

KNET, Visa, Mastercard, Apple Pay and cash on delivery. You can save a card to your account so checkout is one tap next time.

Can I change or cancel an order?

Yes, while the order still shows Order Accepted. Once it moves to Preparing, message us on WhatsApp and we will do what we can.

What if an item is missing or damaged?

Tell us within 24 hours and we refund that item to your original payment method, or replace it on your next delivery — whichever you prefer.

Do you deliver to my area?

We cover Kuwait City, Hawalli, Farwaniya, Ahmadi and Jahra. Pick your area on the address step — if it is on the list, we deliver there.
